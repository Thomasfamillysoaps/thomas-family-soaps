Our Story

At Thomas Family Soaps, every bar is handmade with care, love, and quality ingredients. What started as a family passion quickly turned into something we wanted to share with everyone.

We believe soap should do more than clean — it should make your skin feel refreshed, moisturized, and leave you with amazing scents you love.

Thank you for supporting our family business and shopping small.

Our Ingredients

At Thomas Family Soaps, we carefully select quality ingredients to create handcrafted soaps that leave your skin feeling clean, soft, and refreshed.

  • Coconut Oil
  • Lard
  • Olive Oil
  • Castor Oil
  • Sodium Hydroxide (Lye)
  • Fragrance (depending on the soap)
  • Mica (colorant)

Every bar is made with love and carefully balanced ingredients to create rich lather, lasting scent, and a gentle cleanse.
